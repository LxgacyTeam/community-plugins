# Speedometer Mod

### Plugin authors: [UserRP](https://t.me/NameDayn), 一只 car

Speedometer Mod adds an attractive, customizable speedometer to the game. Choose from four styles, from a classic semicircular dial to a digital display inspired by modern racing games.

## Settings and usage

Choose a position on the left, in the center or on the right of the screen, and select your preferred style. The settings menu supports Russian, English and Chinese.

The menu opens with `Alt+S` by default. You can rebind the shortcut in a few clicks.

## Compatibility

Requires MadOut2 PC 9.4 with BigCityLegacy 1.4.0+.

**Enjoy the game!**
